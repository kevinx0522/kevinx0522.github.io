ClawArena-Team benchmarks the management ability of a language-model agent that creates specialized subagents, delegates work, and orchestrates their parallel, asynchronous returns through dynamic workflows. It comprises 41 multi-turn, multimodal, multi-directory scenarios with 258 evaluation rounds and 72 staged updates. Its execution-based Subagent-Management Score jointly measures task correctness, least-privilege access control, and modality routing.
